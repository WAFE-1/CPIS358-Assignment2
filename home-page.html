About Our Project

Many new students at King Abdulaziz University struggle to find department office hours, lab locations, and registration announcements in one place. Our website solves this problem by collecting basic student links and department schedules on a simple portal.

Main Features:

  • Easy access to FCIT department office hours.
  • Quick announcement board for students.
  • Student login and registration pages.
